\chapter{Feasibility Study}
\label{ch:feas}

\section{Technical Feasibility}

Every part of PurpleAgents is built from existing, proven building blocks. The
target model is a ready-made open-source model. The red and blue sides use
open-source tools. The dual-reasoning engine is built by prompting a reasoning
model and adding simple comparison logic, so no new model needs to be trained.
Connecting to the target is a standard API call. The project is therefore
technically feasible with current, freely available technology.

\section{Economic Feasibility}

All models, tools, datasets, and frameworks used in the project are free and
open-source. The system can run on a student laptop with a local model, or use
the free tier of a cloud-hosted model API. There are no licensing costs. The
project is therefore economically feasible at essentially zero cost.

\section{Schedule and Resource Feasibility}

The work is planned across an eight-month timeline, from M1 to M8, as shown in
Table~\ref{tab:timeline}. Phase~1 (this report) covers the literature survey,
problem definition, and design. The remaining months cover building, testing,
and documenting the system.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Project timeline (M1--M8)}
\label{tab:timeline}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{0.1\textwidth} Y p{0.24\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Month} & \textbf{Activities} & \textbf{Output} \\
\midrule
M1 & Literature survey and problem definition & Research gap, problem statement \\
M2 & System and architecture design & Architecture, algorithm, this report \\
M3 & Environment setup: target model, guardrail toolkit, judge, attack and benign prompt sets & Working safe test environment \\
M4 & Red and blue agent development & Attack and defense modules \\
M5 & Dual-reasoning engine development & Evidence-first, hypothesis-first, convergence \\
M6 & Validation layer, reflection, and auditable memory & Caught/missed check, fix re-testing, audit records \\
M7 & Integration of all components; testing and evaluation over repeated rounds & Measured ASR, DR, false-flag rate \\
M8 & Documentation, CLI demonstration, and final presentation & Final report and demo \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

The team of three will divide the work by module: one member on the red side and
target setup, one on the blue side and validation, and one on the reasoning
engine and memory, with all three sharing integration, evaluation, and
documentation. A team of three students can complete this work within the
available time. The project is therefore feasible in terms of schedule and
resources.
